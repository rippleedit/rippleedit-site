About us

Who you'reworking with.

RippleEdit was founded by Razz, a member of the multi-platinum producer duo STRAIGHTUPGLOBAL. Their credits include Gucci Mane, Offset and Polo G in the US, plus major artists internationally.

Those years inside the industry mean he knows how the craft works at the highest level, and the business behind it just as well. His passion for film editing runs just as deep. It earned him a place at Film Academy Baden-Württemberg, one of Europe's most renowned film universities.

He is using that expertise to build the team behind RippleEdit, whose sole purpose is to bring ideas to life and make them as good as they can be.
